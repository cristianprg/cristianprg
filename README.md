## 🎮 Projects

### 🟢 Ooze of the Arcana

A **top-down 2D boss-rush / bullet-hell prototype** developed in Godot.

You play as a slime fighting through chaotic encounters while dealing with enemy projectiles, special attacks and randomized gameplay elements.

**Worked on:**

* Player movement and dash
* Normal & special attacks
* Boss combat
* Enemy/minion behavior
* Projectile systems
* Randomized stat pickups
* Sound effects and music
* UI and game feedback

> 🎮 A project focused on learning how to turn individual mechanics into a complete playable experience.

---

### 🟡 Crooked Supplier

A **2D narrative/UI-driven game** inspired by games such as *Papers, Please*.

The player works as a strange ingredient distributor, processing customers' requests while uncovering the questionable origin and properties of the products being handled.

The project focuses heavily on:

* UI-driven gameplay
* Game state management
* Data-driven content
* Player decisions
* Inventory and economy systems
* Dark humor and surreal storytelling


Still developing!
